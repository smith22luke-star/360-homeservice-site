A note from Luke & Connor

Thank you, Sammamish.

360 Home Services has closed its doors. It was the best thing either of us has built, and every bit of it came from you.

To our customers, neighbors, and community

What began as a summer break operation turned into something we never expected: a real business, built one front door at a time across the Eastside.

You trusted two college students with your homes. You answered the door, gave us a chance, referred us to your neighbors, and held us to a high standard. We hope we lived up to it.

We're now back at school finishing our degrees, and 360 Home Services is no longer taking jobs or quote requests. Closing was not an easy decision, and we are proud of what this became.

If there is anything pressing, you can reach Luke at smith22luke@gmail.com.

From both of us: thank you for being part of the 360 story.

With gratitude,
Luke & Connor

01

A summer break idea

Two friends home from college, a simple plan: do the work well and treat every home like our own. Pressure washing came first, and the rest followed from there.

02

Door by door

We didn't start with a big marketing budget. We started with a map, a stack of door hangers, and a lot of walking, introducing ourselves in neighborhoods across Sammamish, Bellevue, and Issaquah.

03

Growing with the neighborhood

Gutter cleaning and anti-moss treatment joined the lineup. Repeat customers and referrals kept coming, and entire HOA communities trusted us with their neighborhoods. Past 200 jobs, it stopped feeling like a summer project.

04

Noticed by our neighbors

Local press took notice of two student-athletes running a business between classes and practice. We ended up on the cover of Plateau Neighbors magazine, and Connor was featured by Pacific Lutheran University. Both stories are below.

05

Back to class

This fall we're back at school finishing our degrees, and 360 Home Services has closed. We're grateful for every job, every referral, and every kind word along the way.
