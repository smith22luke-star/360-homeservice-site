A note from Luke & Connor

Thank you, Sammamish.

360 Home Services has closed its doors.

To our customers, neighbors, and community

What began as a summer break operation turned into a real business, built one front door at a time across the Eastside.

You trusted two college students with your homes. You answered the door, gave us a chance, referred us to your neighbors, and held us to a high standard. We hope we lived up to it.

We're now just months away from graduation, and 360 Home Services is no longer taking jobs or quote requests. After school we will continue to work alongside each other on a new opportunity - one that came directly as a result of the success of 360.

We are proud of what we built, and while we're sad to see it go, we are so grateful for the journey, and for everyone who supported us along the way.

If there is anything pressing, you can reach Luke at smith22luke@gmail.com.

From both of us: thank you for being part of the 360 story.

With gratitude,
Luke & Connor
